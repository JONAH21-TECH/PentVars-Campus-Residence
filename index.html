How it works

  1. Create a student account and confirm your active student status.
  2. Pay the GHS 2,000 hall fee (non-refundable) and upload your receipt.
  3. Hall staff and hall executives verify your payment.
  4. Once verified, choose your own room.
  5. Track your room, roommates and any fees from your dashboard.
